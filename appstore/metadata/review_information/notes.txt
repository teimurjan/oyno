Oyno helps a parent put the phone down in the evening. It pauses apps the parent picks on their own iPhone during hours they set (Screen Time API, FamilyControls with .individual authorization), suggests a short game to play with their child, and grows an illustrated planet for every round played.

A demo account with an active entitlement is provided in the credentials fields. Sign in with "Use email instead" on the sign-in screen.

To exercise the core flow: open the Play tab, pick an energy level, tap "Start 10 minutes", then "We're done", then tap the planet. On Home, press the planet to open the gallery of worlds.

To exercise Kid hours: open the Kid hours tab, tap "Allow Screen Time", choose apps, and turn on "Pause apps during Kid hours". The schedule runs in a DeviceActivity monitor extension. Oyno never reads app usage and never collects data about the child.
