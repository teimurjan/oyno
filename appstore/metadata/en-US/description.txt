Be with your kid, not in your phone.

Oyno pauses the apps that pull you in during Kid hours and hands you one easy game to play together, sized to your child's age (0 to 5) and the energy you have left.

GAMES FOR ANY ENERGY
Tell Oyno your battery: low, okay or full. You get one game for 5 to 15 minutes, in three short steps, with nothing to set up. Lie-down games for tired evenings, hide-and-find for the days you have energy. 500 games across three age bands.

EVERY GAME BUILDS A SKILL
Each game exercises an executive function: working memory, self-control, flexible thinking or focus. Each one cites a published study and a child development guide, linked right in the game.

KID HOURS, SET TO THE MINUTE
Make as many schedules as your week needs: school nights, weekend mornings, bath and bed. Each has its own days, times and apps to pause. Pick apps that always stay open and apps that never do. Finish a round to unlock your apps for 1 to 20 minutes, or hold Not now for a 20-minute break.

A PLANET THAT GROWS
Every round you play together grows your family's little planet, and 31 worlds unlock as you play. A skipped day costs nothing: the planet never wilts.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Oyno uses Apple's Screen Time to pause apps. It never sees which apps you use or what you do in them, and it never asks for your child's name or photo.

SUBSCRIPTION
Oyno is a subscription with a monthly and a yearly plan. New subscribers can start with a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when the trial ends, and the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID's subscriptions.
