WHY "THREE TIMES FILAMENT" LOSES YOU MONEY

That rule prices the plastic and nothing else. A 20-hour ASA print draws roughly three times the power of the same print in PLA. Your printer wears out after a finite number of hours. A multi-colour plate can purge more filament into the waste bin than it puts into the model. Etsy takes its cut off the whole order, shipping included.

Oyno prices all of it, in one screen, in about ten seconds.
