Your phone eats the evening with your kid. Oyno takes ten minutes of it back.

KID HOURS
Pick the apps that pull you in and the hours you want to be present, like weekdays from 4:30 to 7:30 PM. During Kid hours those apps pause on your phone. Phone, Messages and Maps always stay open. Need a break? Press and hold Not now to open your apps for 20 minutes.

ONE EASY THING
Tell Oyno how much battery you have left: low, okay or full. It hands you one game for ten minutes, with three simple steps and nothing to set up. Lying-down games for tired evenings, running games for the days you have energy.

YOUR LITTLE PLANET
When the timer ends, your kid taps the planet and it blooms. Every round together grows it, and new worlds unlock as you play. A skipped day costs nothing: the planet never wilts.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Oyno uses Apple's Screen Time to pause apps. It never sees which apps you use or what you do in them, and it never asks for your child's name, age or photo.

SUBSCRIPTION
Oyno is a subscription with a monthly and a yearly plan. New subscribers can start with a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when the trial ends, and the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID's subscriptions.
